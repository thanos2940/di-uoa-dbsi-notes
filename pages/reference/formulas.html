Κόστος Ευρετηρίων

Estimate I/Os for search.

Δομή Κόστος
ISAM \( O(\log_2 p(R) + \text{ovf}) \)
B+ Tree \( O(\log_d n) \)
Ext. Hash \( \approx 1.2 \) I/Os

Join Algorithms

  • Simple NL: \( p_R + t_R \cdot p_S \)
  • Block NL: \( p_R + \lceil \frac{p_R}{M-2} \rceil \cdot p_S \)
  • Sort-Merge: \( Cost_{sort} + p_R + p_S \)
  • Hash Join: \( 3(p_R + p_S) \)
External Sorting

Cost: \( 2 \cdot p(R) \cdot (1 + \lceil \log_{M-1} \lceil p(R)/M \rceil \rceil) \)

  • Phase 0: Produces \(\lceil p(R)/M \rceil\) sorted runs.
  • Merge Phases: We merge \(M-1\) runs at a time.
